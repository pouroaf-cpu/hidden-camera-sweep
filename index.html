Check a room for hidden cameras

Hidden Camera Sweep walks you through a careful look at a room in about ten minutes: the usual hiding spots, a dark scan, a torch scan and a Wi-Fi check. It's made for people who are always away from home, like cabin crew and frequent travellers.

Your scans stay on your phone. No app can prove a room is camera-free, so the report also lists what could not be checked.
