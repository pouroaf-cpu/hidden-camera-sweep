Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Hidden Camera Sweep helps you check a room for hidden cameras. It is built so that what you scan stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

We never receive any of this:

  • The live camera view and the photos of spots the app flags
  • Your reports and the room you picked
  • Results from the Wi-Fi check. The app contacts devices on the network you are connected to, and those results stay on your phone
  • The email address you can save for reports

If you email or share a report, it goes to the people and apps you choose. We are not involved in that.

What leaves your phone

Anonymous usage stats, sent to PostHog. Step counts such as "started a sweep" or "reached the report", whether a pass was skipped, which plan you tried to buy and whether that went through, a rough count of your sweeps, and the app version and platform. Each is tied to a random ID the app makes, not to your name, email or device. Nothing from your scans is included, and PostHog's location lookup is switched off. You can turn this off in Settings under Privacy.

Purchase status, sent to RevenueCat. If you subscribe or restore a purchase, RevenueCat records which plan you hold against a random ID, so the app knows reports are unlocked. Payment is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up
  • No selling of data
  • No advertising or tracking across other apps and websites
  • No location data

Keeping and deleting data

Delete your reports and photos on the phone at any time under Settings, Delete all reports. Deleting the app removes them too. To ask us to delete anonymous usage stats or purchase records, contact us at the address below.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
